What the numbers say

The library is wide but shallow. 56 of 123 cases (46%) are marked automated and 57 scripts exist, yet only 24 distinct automated cases (19.5%) have ever been reached by a recorded execution — and just 20 (16.3%) passed. The gap between "automated" and "executed" is where coverage is actually being lost, not in authoring.

Risk sits in the wrong place. Critical cases are 32 of 123 (26% of the suite) but hold 1 automated script (3.1%); high is 54% and medium 71%. Requirement coverage reads 100% (20/20) while 0 requirements are backed by an automated case — largely because 55 cases carry no requirement link at all, so the two percentages measure different things and should be read separately.

Execution is the binding constraint. 33 of 57 scripts have no recorded run, and the newest execution of any kind is 16 Nov (a single local script) with the newest suite run on 14 Nov (Sanity Test 9/10, 90%; Self Check 3/4, 75% on 9 Oct). Self Check and Sanity are the only two suites defined, so most of the library is outside any repeatable run.

The red set is one bug, not eleven. 10 of the 11 failing scripts die inside the shared admin sign-in path (getByTestId('username'), 15s) covering TC-37…TC-46 — all six admin-dashboard cases and four admin-settings cases. The eleventh (Manager login, unlinked) is blocked by the promo-overlay intercepting the submit click. Additionally, 7 scripts flip outcomes between runs (flakiness 22–50%), 4 of them in tests/shop, so shop greens are not trustworthy yet.

Priority order: fix the admin auth locator and re-run the admin/profile specs (recovers 10 scripts and 10 test cases); link the 28 unlinked scripts so their results count; run or retire the 33 never-run scripts starting with tests/forms (6) and tests/dynamic (5); automate critical cases before adding more medium-priority breadth.
